# RYM_VARGANI V1.6.5

- Receipt Centre and 80G Receipt Centre now include a search bar.
- Receipts are sorted by receipt number from higher to lower using numeric-aware sorting.
- Fully collected donors are hidden from Route Collection.
- In the Donors list, fully collected donors remain visible but Collect is disabled and replaced by “Donation Collected”.
- The collection modal is also guarded against being opened for an already-collected donor.
- No Supabase schema upgrade is required for this release.
